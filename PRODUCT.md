# TelcoPulse

## Register

product

## Users
Telecom ITOC operators, incident commanders, and engineers monitoring customer journeys and investigating service degradation.

## Product Purpose
Connect customer transactions to operational evidence, incidents, mitigation, resolution, and learning. The complete requirements are in docs/PROJECT_GOAL.md.

## Brand Personality
Precise, composed, accountable. Enterprise internal tooling with a telecom crimson identity.

## Anti-references
Portfolio dashboards, decorative card collections, excessive gradients or glow, untouched component-library templates, and misleading healthy states.

## Design Principles
- Prioritize affected services and customer impact.
- Correlate evidence through transaction and trace IDs.
- Make operational changes explicit and auditable.
- Keep dense information scannable and statuses truthful.
- Show useful empty and failure states instead of fabricated live data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard navigation, visible focus, accessible contrast, text alongside semantic colors, responsive layouts, reduced motion, and labeled form validation.
